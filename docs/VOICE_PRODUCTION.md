## 3. Delivering recordings
1. **Record** to the script's spec: mono, 48 kHz, peak −3 dBFS, ~150 ms of room tone at each end, no music or effects.
2. **Encode** each file to Ogg Vorbis q5 at its exact path, `public/assets/audio/voice/<character>/<line id>.ogg`.
   - Extra takes, for variety: `<id>.2.ogg` … `<id>.4.ogg`. The game alternates takes and never plays the same take twice in a row.
   - Rough variants: `<id>.rough.ogg`. If a line has no rough recording, the family take plays.
3. **Credit the performer.** Add one entry per recorded character to `credits.json`:
   ```json
   [{ "character": "thief", "performer": "<name>", "source": "human-recording", "licence": "work-for-hire, all rights to the project", "date": "2026-12-01" }]
   ```
   `source` must be `human-recording` or `licensed-synthetic`. Synthetic voices must never be credited as a human performer.
4. **Regenerate the manifest.** `npm run voice:manifest` writes `available.json` and refuses uncredited files.
5. **Update the script.** `npm run voice:script` marks recorded lines with ✓.
6. **What the runtime then does:** it fetches only listed files and decodes each on first use. It preloads all priority-3 lines (alarms and the catch) so their first play is voiced. Subtitle length follows the real duration of the recording.

## 4. Voice-generation service check (read-only, no cost)
A generation service (Higgsfield) is connected to this workspace. **Nothing was generated and no credits were spent.** Read-only queries (2026-11-13) found:

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Engines offered | ElevenLabs Eleven v4 / v4 Turbo, Qwen Audio 3.0 TTS, Inworld TTS, a multi-engine "Text to Speech V2" (ElevenLabs, MiniMax, Seed Speech, Vibe Voice, Cozy Voice) |
| Bengali | **Not listed** for Qwen (its language hints exclude Bengali) or Inworld (no Bengali voice). The preset voice list is English-named. ElevenLabs is multilingual, but Bengali quality, and especially rural dialect delivery, **was not verified** |
| Pricing | Credit-based. A pricing widget exists, but per-line costs were not looked up beyond that, and nothing was purchased |
| Licensing | Commercial use of generated voices depends on the provider's terms and the plan. Must be confirmed **in writing** before use in a distributed game |
| Permissions | **Requires the owner's explicit approval** before any paid generation |

**Recommendation.** Use **human voice actors** for the thief, Rahim, Majnu and the merchants: dialect, comic timing and the screams carry the game's humour. A synthetic voice is at best a temporary scratch track, credited as `licensed-synthetic`.

## 5. Mixing guidance
Voice sits on its own bus (Settings → সংলাপ).
- **NPC lines:** spatial, with a 34 m roll-off.
- **Thief lines:** in the head (no panning).
- **Crowd shouts:** the catch scene is the only place lines may overlap.
- **Loudness:** keep takes −16 to −14 LUFS-integrated per line, so barks don't jump over the ambience.
